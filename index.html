02 / The process

From structure
to feeling.

The goal was not simply to make the screens look polished. It was to make the experience easier to understand, more personal, and welcoming to a wider range of users.

PHASE 01

Map the essentials

Low-fidelity wireframes helped establish the main screens and journeys, including mood tracking, music recommendations, and mindfulness exercises.

PHASE 02

Build the visual language

The high-fidelity direction introduced a cohesive palette, typography, iconography, and stronger information hierarchy to make the product feel more complete.

PHASE 03

Design with inclusion in mind

Accessibility considerations informed readable text, customizable text sizes, voice-to-text and voice-command concepts, and guided audio experiences.

03 / Design principles

Small details.
More thoughtful
experiences.

For a product connected to emotional well-being, the interface should feel supportive rather than demanding. These principles guided the design direction.

♡

Lead with empathy

Make mood check-ins feel simple and non-judgmental, giving people an approachable way to reflect on how they feel.

↗

Keep navigation clear

Give key actions—checking in, finding music, and starting an exercise—a clear place in the experience.

◌

Make it personal

Connect mood input with relevant music and mindfulness options so the experience feels tailored to the moment.

Aa

Design for access

Consider legible typography, flexible text sizing, and audio or voice-based ways to engage with the app.

04 / Reflection

Good design is an ongoing conversation.

Moving from wireframes to a high-fidelity prototype reinforced the value of iteration: early structure creates a foundation, while thoughtful refinement helps the experience communicate more clearly. Mindful Melodies brought together visual consistency, interaction design, and accessibility considerations around one central idea—make it easier for people to find a moment of calm. The project also strengthened my interest in the space where technology, human behavior, and meaningful product experiences meet.
